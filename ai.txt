# UrukCode

Live: https://umaer-islam.github.io/urukcode/
Repo: https://github.com/umaer-islam/urukcode
Developer: Umaer Islam (https://umaerislam.com)
License: MIT

Static web app. QR code generator (URL, text, WiFi, vCard, email, phone, SMS, geo). Barcode generator (CODE128, EAN-13, UPC, CODE39, CODE93, EAN-8, UPC-E, ITF-14, ITF, MSI, Pharmacode, Codabar). QR decoder from image upload. Export PNG/SVG. Client-side only. No data leaves the browser.
